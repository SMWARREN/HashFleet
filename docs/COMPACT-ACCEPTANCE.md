## Interpretation and recovery limits

The literal original turn was unavailable after oversized encrypted compaction. Scope was recovered from the existing acceptance plan, worker assignments and implemented navigation, rather than from a new confirmation by the user. The entire recovered implementation, including work done before that recovery, was included in the final native run and the requirement mapping above.

Interpretations that were not separately user-confirmed:

- **Compact / miners at the top:** a complete first miner card must fit in the default phone viewport below a compact solo comparison. Large accessibility text may require scrolling rather than shrinking or truncating content.
- **Marquee:** a four-second cycling miner status strip with pause, not a continuously moving horizontal crawl. Accessibility settings use static full summaries.
- **Tuning:** make existing reviewed frequency/voltage forms easy to reach, not invent safe overclock profiles or apply settings automatically.
- **Coin / shared-pool setup:** documented SHA-256d choices and official solo/shared endpoint drafts, not arbitrary tokens, a profitability engine or automatic miner reconfiguration.

The native before/after result is measurably better on the compactness criterion above. That does not establish the user's subjective preference or physical-phone usability. The initial phone-lock barrier was resolved by the owner at 06:06 UTC. Remote launch succeeded at 06:07 UTC and the process remained present at 06:08 UTC (`build/compact-acceptance/phone-unlocked-launch.log`, `phone-processes.json`). This establishes installation and launch, not visual inspection of the physical phone or validation against its real fleet.

## Baseline and boundaries

- `build/compact-acceptance/before.png`: previous native app on the iPhone 17 Pro simulator, with no miner in the initial viewport.
- Targeted native screenshots now show a complete miner card and part of the second card. Miner details expose Tuning, Cooling and Pools above secondary disclosures.
- The ticker uses a cancellable four-second task. Pause, scene inactivity, disappearance and offscreen geometry stop advancement. Reduce Motion, VoiceOver and accessibility text sizes use static, fully wrapping summaries. No ticker networking is added.
- UI transport checks use an IPv4 loopback server and the real app/URLSession. They do not write to a physical miner. Demo checks are never presented as live miner evidence.
- Presets are local drafts until full-slot replacement acknowledgment, exact JSON review and final APPLY confirmation. Unknown pool adapters gain no invented account support.
- ASIC model recognition identifies a hashing algorithm, not guaranteed firmware/pool support or a hardware-safe voltage range. There are no guessed overclock profiles.
- BTC/BCH prices use existing public sources. BC2/BCH2/XEC prices explicitly remain unavailable. Fees, payout thresholds and profitability are not invented.
