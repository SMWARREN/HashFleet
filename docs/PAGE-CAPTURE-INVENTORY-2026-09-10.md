## Counting and limitations

Diagnostics, history, offline settings, disclosure expansions and light appearances are states, not separate top-level routes. Endpoint catalog operations share a generic editor, with representative read, PATCH, restart and binary-upload variants captured. Native document pickers, share sheets, alerts and system Siri/Control Center surfaces are not app-designed pages and are not exhaustively captured.

56 screenshot files; 33 grouped route families and explicit states; 10 generated images. These are different counts and must not be conflated.
