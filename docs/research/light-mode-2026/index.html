My recommendation: A · Warm Graphite

Warm the canvas slightly, keep white cards above it, retain the charcoal hashrate card, and use the icon gold as an accent rather than small text. This preserves the depth you asked for without turning light mode into beige-on-beige or every card into a colored panel.

Keep meaning stable. Green means online or positive. Amber means caution, accompanied by words or a symbol. Coin icons keep their own identities. Dark mode and the approved app artwork stay unchanged in every proposal.

Dynamic and composited colors

Native .primary, .secondary, system orange, tint, materials and disabled controls are resolved by iOS. They do not have one trustworthy fixed hex across appearance, accessibility settings and backgrounds. White/black opacity overlays, badge fills at 9%, default card outlines at 4.5%, and comparison outlines at 25% also depend on what is behind them. The inventory retains those call sites rather than inventing a flat palette value.

Separate surfaces and artwork

Widgets still have their own older cream/orange palette. Onboarding uses peach, and live logs use translucent white text on charcoal. The fourth chart series uses slate blue. About uses system orange. These need an explicit follow-up decision if you choose a palette. AppIcon, HashFleetBrand and five bundled coin images are artwork, not flat UI colors. Their pixel palettes remain untouched.

What the research supports

  • Apple Design Awards · June 2, 2026: cohesive themes, readable data, platform-appropriate controls and accessibility. There is no universal “2026 palette.”
  • Envato · February 5, 2026: calm neutrals, expressive accents and high contrast are relevant directions. This is trend commentary and template marketing, not an accessibility standard.
  • W3C contrast guidance: 4.5:1 for ordinary text, 3:1 for qualifying large text. All 15 tested text-role pairs per proposal exceed 4.5:1. Decorative outlines are intentionally subtle and are not the sole signifier of a control.

Contrast caveat: the icon gold #E9921D against white is only 2.44:1. Keep it for fills, artwork and decoration. Use shaded gold for small links and actions. These checks cover opaque color pairs, not every native UI state, translucent material, chart or image.
