One miner. Two native modes.

Colors sampled from the exact single-miner dark logo and the supplied ivory-background version. The original artwork is preserved. These are screenshots of the running SwiftUI app in its labeled, read-only demo, not generated mockups.

Color decisions

  • No pure-white or pure-black custom canvas, card, ticker or text tokens. Native iOS chrome, shadows and the unchanged artwork can still contain black or white.
  • Raw gold is used for the hashrate surface, not small text on ivory. Bronze makes light-mode actions readable.
  • Dark action gold is a documented blend of the casing and display glow. It remains legible even on the brightest graphite surface.
  • Sage is a functional online/success companion, not a claimed logo extraction. Status labels and symbols preserve meaning without color alone.

62 specified contrast checks pass. Lowest tested normal-text contrast: 4.60:1 (minimum 4.5:1). Control-boundary and fourth-chart-series roles are checked at 3:1. Decorative low-opacity outlines are not presented as accessible control boundaries.
