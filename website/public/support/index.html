A little help.

Technical support for Hash Fleet on iPhone, iPad and Mac. Contact the developer with app questions, a bug report, a privacy question or a data-removal request.

For a useful bug report

  • Tell us whether you use iPhone, iPad or Mac, your operating-system version and the Hash Fleet app version.
  • Describe what you expected, what happened and the steps that reproduce the problem.
  • If a miner is involved, include its model and firmware version. Available readings and controls vary by hardware.
  • Review screenshots and logs before sharing. Remove miner addresses, wallet details and other private information.
Keep your hardware and credentials safe. Never send passwords, private keys, recovery phrases or unredacted miner configuration. Use a trusted VPN for remote access, not a publicly exposed miner API.

Questions about data? Read the Hash Fleet privacy policy. This page has no contact form, tracking scripts or account signup. The email link opens your own mail app only when you choose it.
